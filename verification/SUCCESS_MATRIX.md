**`balakumar_malik1992_branches`** (Second (Mack) mode) — 🟡 acceptable (branch topology: separation of the discrete second mode from the continuous-spectrum eigenvalues in the complex-alpha plane)  

pyMack's full spatial spectrum at the verified B&M (1992) M4.5 condition (M=4.5, T0=311 K, Re=1000, omega=0.2, adiabatic) reproduces B&M's QUALITATIVE topology: (1) the discrete SECOND mode at alpha=0.220210-0.002787i (c=0.9082) matches the published 0.220-0.003091i (alpha_r 0.10%, alpha_i 9.8%) and is the UNIQUE least-damped root in the second-mode phase-speed band (every other in-band root is more damped by >= 0.0193 in alpha_i; the only 3 more-amplified roots are spurious high-|alpha| / low-c companion artifacts, not a competing second mode); (2) a continuous-spectrum cluster of 93 roots accumulates at the freestream phase speed c->1 (alpha_r->omega), median alpha_i=+0.0091, plus an acoustic ladder at c>1 -- B&M's continuous-spectrum branches. HONEST CAVEAT: the separation is in the phase-speed/growth-rate sense, NOT a crisp Euclidean gap. The discretized continuum is dense in alpha_r, so the nearest other eigenvalue is only 0.019 away (sep ratio 0.09) and 42 roots fall within 10% of |alpha_disc|. This is the discretization signature of the continuous spectrum crowding the discrete mode in alpha_r, not a spurious second mode -- hence 'acceptable' (topology correct) rather than 'agrees' (which would need a clean Euclidean gap). B&M present the branches graphically; no tabulated branch eigenvalues are openly recoverable, so this is a qualitative branch-topology check.

**`cone_sivasubramanian_fasel_2015`** (Second (Mack) mode) — 🟡 acceptable (second-mode peak-N frequency (kHz) and peak linear N-factor on the sharp cone, integrated over the SAME physical extent as thesis Fig. 5.1 (lower neutral point -> domain end))  

PRIOR-VERSION ERROR CORRECTED. The previous verdict used loose secondary values (peak N~7-8, most-amplified band 220-280 kHz) citing the JFM paper generically; the 220-280 kHz band actually came from a different Reynolds condition in the design review, not from this case. The ORIGINAL source -- Sivasubramanian (2012) PhD thesis Fig. 5.1, the linear parent of Sivasubramanian & Fasel (2015) JFM 768 -- states VERBATIM that the most-amplified axisymmetric wave (kc=0) reaches N~9.5 and has F=1.071e-5 (f*~210 kHz). The benchmark is therefore peak N~9.5 at f~210 kHz, NOT 7-8 / 220-280 kHz. DOMAIN-MATCHED RE-INTEGRATION: the thesis integrates N=ln(A/A0) from each wave's lower neutral point over its x* domain (axial 0.30->0.59 m, into the x*L=0.6 m buffer). Mapping that physical extent to pyMack's surface s=x*/cos(7deg) and R_eq=sqrt(Re_s/3) with the same Taylor-Maccoll edge state, and integrating from the lower neutral point (clip_negative, matching A0), pyMack's peak-N frequency is 205 kHz (N=8.11) with N=8.05 evaluated at the thesis 210 kHz. FREQUENCY: 210 vs 210 kHz is an EXACT match (2.4% if read at pyMack's own 205 kHz peak) -- pyMack independently reproduces F=1.071e-5 as the most-amplified frequency. N-FACTOR: pyMack 8.0-8.1 vs thesis 9.5 is ~15% short. The shape tracks the thesis well through x*~0.50 m (pyMack 6.5 vs ~6.6) but the self-similar Blasius/Mangler local growth rate decays faster than the cone DNS in the back half (x*>0.47 m: pyMack sigma falls from 4.3e-3 to 0.6e-3), so the last ~1.4 of N is not accumulated. This is the expected signature of omitting transverse curvature and the real non-similar cone mean flow. The domain-matched N (8.05) is a clear improvement over the prior loose-window 7.06, and is cross-validated by the BAM6QT design review (N~8 at end of cone, ~9 at end of flare, >10 low-noise -- bracketing 9.5). VERDICT = acceptable: frequency agrees exactly, N sits right at the 15% boundary with a bounded, physically-understood Mangler/self-similar deficit; no topology/mode mismatch (clean discrete second mode, single growth lobe, monotone N from the lower neutral point).
